📚 Mathematical Standards Reference Guide (Year 4 KSSR)
Standard 6.3: Perimeter & Area

• Perimeter of Rectangle: Perimeter = 2 × (Length + Width)
• Area of Rectangle & Square: Area = Length × Width
• Area of Triangle: Area = ½ × Base × Perpendicular Height
• Perimeter measured in linear units (m, cm).
• Area measured in square units (m², cm²).

Standard 6.4.1: Volume

• Cuboid: Volume = Length × Width × Height
• Cube: Volume = Side × Side × Side
• Volume is expressed in cubic units (cm³, m³).
